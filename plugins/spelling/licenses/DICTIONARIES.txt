Shotto Office spelling dictionaries
Source package: Debian libreoffice-dictionaries 7.5.0-1
https://deb.debian.org/debian/pool/main/libr/libreoffice-dictionaries/
Binary packages: hunspell-sw and hunspell-en-gb. The original dictionary and
affix files are supplied unmodified in dictionaries/. The same text is
embedded in dictionaries.js (decoded as ISO-8859-1 for sw_TZ and UTF-8 for
en_GB). A short additional list of Swahili verb forms is built in plugin.js.
